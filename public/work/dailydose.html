0 1 T H E B R I E F / /

Self-care for a modern day.

The brief
What Sista needed

Sista, an established brand, wanted to launch a new line of dietary supplements in the self-care and longevity space.

DailyDose was for people with modern lives: a routine that shouldn't take much time, with results they can clearly see.

0 2 T H E I D E A / /

An aspiration, not an obligation.

The idea
Prevention, made easy

Self-care and longevity are more than a trend. People take them on because prevention pays off over a long, good life.

So the brand starts from a simple belief: looking after yourself should feel easy, even fun. Something you want to do, not something you have to.

0 3 T H E J O U R N E Y / /

Let the capsules carry the colour.

The journey
What I did

The design took a minimalist route. Colour, usually the backbone of a visual identity, was taken out of the brand and packaging graphics, leaving typography to carry the identity on its own.

The colour moved to the product itself: frosted bottles that let vivid capsules show through, one hue per formula. That's where the colour shines, and where it's most useful.

The result is clean, clear and easy to tell apart at a glance, designed for adults and seniors who want function, not ornament.
