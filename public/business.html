0 2 A P P R O A C H / /

Diagnose first. Then design.

Every engagement follows the same four moves, whether it lasts three weeks or a year. You always know which one we are in, and what it will produce.

Step01

Diagnose

Interviews, data, the real numbers. You get a written diagnosis, not a slide of adjectives.

Step02

Design

The strategy, structure or plan, built with your team so they can run it without me.

Step03

Deploy

I stay for the first cycle: the briefs, the hires, the tools, the launch.

Step04

Measure

A dashboard and a monthly review. If it isn't moving, we change it.
